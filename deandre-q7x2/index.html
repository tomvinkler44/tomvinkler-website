What I noticed

My read so far, from your résumé and LinkedIn

As a marketer, your LinkedIn and résumé are the first piece of your work a hiring manager sees. Right now they don't look like what a senior marketer would produce, and I think that explains your “not getting interviews” complaint.

Why interviews are harder to get

  • Your LinkedIn photo isn't helping. Recruiters check your profile before they invite you. A photo that doesn't look professional can end it right there.
  • Your profile doesn't say “digital marketing.” The headline is generic and your posts don't show that this is what you live and breathe.
  • Your Experience section works against you. It lists membership entries that don't belong there, and your jobs have no description, so your actual work isn't visible.
  • Your résumé is hard to scan. No summary, no clear target role, and the strongest results are buried inside long bullets.

Why first rounds aren't turning into next rounds

  • Results read as activity, not business impact. Most of your numbers are audience growth and engagement. Hiring managers at the next level listen for leads, conversions and revenue.
  • You may be coming across as a doer, not a strategist. “More experience” often means they didn't hear ownership: decisions you made, budgets, stakeholders, trade-offs.
  • There may be gaps in what's current. Paid media, analytics and attribution, AI in marketing. If there are gaps, screening questions find them fast, so I'd check early.
  • Your stories may not be landing. Answers that don't clearly state the problem, your decision and the result lose people within minutes.

I can't tell yet which of these matter most. The first couple of sessions will show us, and then we fix them in order.
