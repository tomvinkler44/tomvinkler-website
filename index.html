How I help

With you for the whole search, start to signed offer

This is a tough job market, and a strategy only works if you understand it and follow it. So I don't hand you a plan and disappear. I walk you through each step, explain why it matters, check that it's working, and adjust it with you when it isn't. You'll always know what to do next.

Job search

Get seen by the people who hire

Most applications vanish because they look like the other 900. I show you how recruiters actually screen, then build a search that reaches hiring managers directly.

  • Target list of employers worth your time
  • Outreach messages that get replies
  • Resume and LinkedIn written for a recruiter's 30-second scan
  • Interview prep and salary negotiation
Direction in the AI era

Figure out what's next, and what lasts

AI is changing which jobs exist and what they ask of people. We work out what you want, which of your skills carry over, and where they'll still be valuable in five years.

  • Strengths and interests, mapped to real roles
  • Transferable-skills map for your pivot
  • Short experiments to test a direction before committing
  • Using AI tools well, and knowing where they mislead
Calm and joy

Stay steady through the search

A long search wears people down: the silence, the rejections, the money worry. We build a rhythm you can sustain, and look for the parts of work that actually make you happy.

  • A weekly routine with real stopping points
  • Handling rejection and long silences
  • Networking that works for introverts
  • Finding meaning in the job you take next

The philosophy behind every session

Cut the noise, elevate the signal.

From the hiring side, most job searches are noise: hundreds of identical applications nobody reads. A good search sends fewer, clearer signals to the right people.

NoiseSignal
Easy Apply as applicant #945A short note to someone on the team
"Results-driven team player"One specific result, with a number
Self-rated skills listA small piece of work that shows the skill

Questions

Before you book

What are my payment options?

Pay in full by card, or choose Affirm at checkout to spread the cost. Acceleration can also be paid in 2 payments of $1,250, and Executive Partnership in 3 payments of $1,550. Affirm's approval and terms are set by Affirm, and checking your options won't affect your credit score.

Do you guarantee I'll get a job?

No one honestly can. What the top package at each level does guarantee is interviews: four with hiring managers within 75 days on Acceleration, or four with decision-makers within 90 days on Executive Partnership, as long as you keep up your weekly outreach. If we miss that, I rework your materials at no charge and keep coaching you until you sign an offer.

Are sessions online or in person?

Online by video, so you can be anywhere. In-person sessions are available in the South Bay on request.

Who do you work with?

Mid-level professionals, managers and senior individual contributors through to directors, VPs and executives. Tech and corporate backgrounds especially, since that's where I recruited.

What if I'm not sure which package fits?

Book the free 20-minute call, or start with the smallest package at your level. If you upgrade within 7 days, everything you paid counts toward the larger package.

About

Hi, I'm Tom

For six years I was a senior recruiter and recruitment manager, screening candidates and watching which ones got hired, and why. Before that I was a corporate trainer and coach; after it, a program manager at companies including Uber and Cisco. My master's is in counseling psychology.

Since 2021 I've coached people through job searches and career changes, and helped design the coaching approach behind an AI job-search platform. I wrote Zen and the Art of Job Hunting because most job-search advice ignores how stressful the process is. I think you search better when you're steadier.
